Alignment guardrails in production assistants often include toxicity screening, bias detection, prompt injection checks, and policy-based blocking. These controls should run before and after generation. Input guardrails detect unsafe requests, while output guardrails detect harmful or misaligned model responses.

A useful risk scoring layer combines content safety signals with grounding signals. For example, a system may lower trust when retrieval confidence is weak, when citation coverage is incomplete, or when the answer contains generalized claims about protected groups.

Guardrails do not replace retrieval quality. They reduce unsafe behavior, but factual reliability still depends on evidence retrieval, source diversity, and answer validation.
